About

Ozark Wire is a high-signal briefing desk for people who track Bitcoin and hard assets, gold, copper, and metals, energy and refined products, global liquidity (Howell / Bhatia — levels and second derivatives), Austrian / sound money, geopolitics (Ukraine, Europe, Middle East), and intel / deep-state adjacent reporting — not entertainment.

The beat is simple: more context, less noise. Numbered items. Category chips. Tight bullets. Prices on the masthead (BTC with chain height/fee, gold with Shanghai prem/disc under it, copper, CME live cattle LE, WTI/Brent, 10Y, MUEL when quoted). Odds and talks on the rails. The map is part of the story.

Extra watchlines baked into the desk (not separate nav): Shanghai gold premium / discount under GOLD (SGE/SHAU vs COMEX — China physical demand / arb), live cattle futures (CME LE), EU politics / elections (France, UK timing, NL when markets reopen), diesel / refined cracks and Hormuz risk premium (Brent–WTI), Treasury / 10Y, and liquidity second derivatives — not just the stock of liquidity.

  • Model Portfolio — public holdings desk: symbol, sector, weight %, last price, day %; YTD % chart vs SPX. No dollar totals.
